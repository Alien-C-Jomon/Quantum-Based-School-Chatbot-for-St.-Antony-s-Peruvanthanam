Q-AI Quantum Experiment Log
===========================

Experiment 1: Single-qubit quantum scoring

Question:
Who is the HOD of Computer Science?

Dataset:
43 faculty records

Classical execution time:
0.000507 seconds

Quantum simulation execution time:
0.428603 seconds

Classical winner:
Mrs. Jintumol John

Quantum winner:
Mrs. Jintumol John

Observation:
Both methods identified the same top candidate.
The local quantum simulation was substantially slower than the
classical method.

Reason:
The experiment used Qiskit Aer to simulate quantum measurements
on a classical CPU. The circuit was not designed as a quantum
speedup algorithm.

Conclusion:
No quantum speedup was demonstrated in this experiment.
Further experiments are required using a quantum search algorithm
and larger problem sizes.
